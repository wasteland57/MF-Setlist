Connect your song list

  1. Row 1 holds headers. Required: Title, Length, Notes. Optional: Artist, Band.
  2. Length accepts 4:12, 4 (minutes), 252 (seconds), or a real Sheets duration cell.
  3. In Sheets: Share → General access → Anyone with the link → Viewer. Without this the load fails.
  4. Paste the sheet address up top, and put the tab name in the Tab box (e.g. Master). Leave it blank and it reads the leftmost tab.

A Band column turns on a band filter, so all your projects can share one sheet. Paste CSV is always there as a manual fallback.
